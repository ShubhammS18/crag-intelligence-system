UAE LABOR LAW AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS - DIFC UPDATE - JANUARY 2026

The Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) is a special economic zone established in 2004 in the heart of Dubai. It covers 110 hectares and serves as the leading financial hub for the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia (MEASA) region. The DIFC operates as an independent jurisdiction with its own civil and commercial laws, courts, and regulatory framework, entirely separate from the wider UAE legal system.

=== SECTION 1: AI AND TECH COMPANY REGULATIONS ===

The DIFC has introduced new regulations specifically for AI and technology companies effective January 2026.

1. All AI startups in DIFC must appoint a "Data Ethics Officer" by Q3 2026.
2. The corporate tax rate for Tech Startups with revenue under 3M AED is 0%.
3. New Visa Rules: AI Engineers with 2+ years of experience are eligible for the 10-year Golden Visa immediately.
4. Working Hours: The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 4.5 days (ending Friday at 12:00 PM).
5. All DIFC-registered AI companies must comply with the DFSA data protection framework, which was updated in January 2026 to include stricter rules on data residency and cross-border data transfers.
6. DIFC has been selected to host the Global Privacy Assembly in 2026, reflecting its leading role in data protection regulation in the region.

=== SECTION 2: CORPORATE TAX FRAMEWORK ===

The UAE introduced a federal corporate tax regime effective June 2023. Under this regime:

- The standard corporate tax rate is 9% on taxable income exceeding AED 375,000.
- Taxable income up to AED 375,000 is taxed at 0%.
- Qualifying Free Zone Persons (QFZPs) are eligible for a 0% corporate tax rate on qualifying income.
- To maintain QFZP status, companies must meet economic substance requirements and maintain proper income tracking.
- If a Free Zone Person fails to meet QFZP conditions in a tax period, the 0% treatment is lost for that period and the subsequent four tax periods.
- Tech startups in DIFC with revenue under 3 million AED qualify for the 0% corporate tax rate under the startup exemption.
- DIFC offers companies a 50-year guarantee of zero taxes on corporate income and profits under the free zone framework.
- Companies must prepare and maintain audited financial statements as part of meeting QFZP conditions.

=== SECTION 3: EMPLOYMENT LAW ===

The primary law governing employment in the DIFC is the DIFC Employment Law No. 2 of 2019, as amended up to 2025. Key provisions include:

WORKING HOURS:
- Maximum working hours are 48 hours per week, with a maximum of 8 hours per day.
- Employees are entitled to at least one full rest day per week, ideally Friday.
- The standard work week for DIFC companies ends at 12:00 PM on Friday (4.5 days).
- Employees must receive 11 consecutive hours off per day.
- Overtime is not expressly mandated as paid under DIFC law; it depends on the employment contract.

ANNUAL LEAVE AND SICK LEAVE:
- After completing one year of service, employees are entitled to 30 calendar days of annual leave.
- Employees with at least 90 days of service are entitled to 20 working days of paid leave annually.
- Unused annual leave can be carried forward but expires after a maximum of 12 months.
- Sick leave entitlement: 100% of daily wage for the first 10 workdays of sick leave in a 12-month period.
- Sick leave entitlement: 50% of daily wage for the next 20 workdays in the same 12-month period.

EMPLOYMENT CONTRACTS:
- Employers must provide written employment contracts in English within seven days of commencement.
- Contracts must specify job title, start date, salary, notice period, and leave entitlements.
- Probation period is capped at six months.
- Wages must be paid at least once per month with itemised pay statements.

END OF SERVICE BENEFITS - DEWS SCHEME:
- DIFC replaced the traditional end-of-service gratuity with the DIFC Employee Workplace Savings (DEWS) Plan in 2020.
- Employers must contribute monthly to the DEWS scheme for each employee.
- Minimum contribution: 5.83% of basic salary for employees with less than five years of service.
- Contribution increases to 8.33% for employees with five or more years of service.
- Failure to enrol employees or pay contributions can lead to fines of up to USD 2,000 per employee.
- End-of-service payments must be made within 14 days of termination.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION:
- The DIFC Courts have exclusive jurisdiction over employment matters within the free zone.
- The Small Claims Tribunal handles claims up to AED 500,000 and is designed for straightforward disputes.
- Mediation is encouraged and many disputes are resolved before reaching formal trial.

=== SECTION 4: BUSINESS SETUP AND COMPANY STRUCTURES ===

OWNERSHIP AND LICENSING:
- DIFC offers 100% foreign ownership without the need for a local UAE partner.
- No restrictions on foreign exchange or capital and profit repatriation.
- DIFC registered 1,081 new active companies between January and June 2025, a 32% increase compared to the same period in 2024.
- As of end of 2024, DIFC hosts more than 6,920 active companies.
- DIFC is home to more than 260 banking and capital markets companies, 410 wealth and asset management firms, and 125 insurance companies.

VARIABLE CAPITAL COMPANY (VCC) REGULATIONS 2026:
- The DIFC Authority enacted new Variable Capital Company (VCC) Regulations on 9 February 2026.
- VCCs can be established as standalone companies or as umbrella structures with incorporated or segregated cells.
- The VCC regime removes previous qualifying purpose restrictions, making flexible corporate forms broadly available.
- Exempt VCCs include those controlled by DIFC Registered Persons, Authorised Firms, or government entities.
- VCCs are permitted to make dividend distributions from capital based on net asset value.
- Share capital in VCCs is aligned with net asset value, enabling efficient issuance and redemption of shares.

LEGAL FRAMEWORK:
- DIFC follows the common law framework and operates in English.
- DIFC laws default to English law in cases of ambiguity.
- The DIFC Courts have judges from common law jurisdictions including England, Singapore, and Hong Kong.
- The regulatory body is the Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA), an independent regulator exclusive to DIFC.

=== SECTION 5: CRYPTO AND DIGITAL ASSETS REGULATIONS ===

The DFSA updated its crypto token regulations effective 12 January 2026:

- Firms providing financial services involving crypto tokens must now assess tokens themselves, replacing the DFSA's previous list of Recognised Crypto Tokens.
- Companies must disclose and demonstrate their monitoring processes for tokens they have deemed suitable.
- Assessment factors include regulatory status in other jurisdictions, trading history, underlying technology, and governance structures.
- The updated framework aligns DIFC with other leading international regimes including ADGM.
- DFSA prudential amendments affecting capital requirements are scheduled for 1 July 2026.
- Companies using contractors in Dubai must also plan for the UAE e-invoicing rollout.

=== SECTION 6: VISA AND RESIDENCY RULES FOR PROFESSIONALS ===

UAE GOLDEN VISA PROGRAMME:
- AI Engineers with 2 or more years of professional experience are eligible for the 10-year Golden Visa immediately upon application through DIFC.
- The Golden Visa provides long-term residency without requiring employer sponsorship renewal.
- Golden Visa holders can sponsor family members including spouse and children.
- Data scientists, machine learning engineers, and software engineers specialising in AI are included in the eligible categories.
- The Golden Visa for tech professionals does not require a minimum salary threshold under the DIFC pathway.

STANDARD WORK VISA:
- Standard employment visas are tied to employer sponsorship.
- Visa processing through DIFC typically takes 5 to 10 working days.
- Medical fitness tests and Emirates ID registration are required for all visa holders.
